# Subscriptions Management

Manage individual subscription records, communicate with subscribers, and work with subscriptions in bulk from the Joomla administrator area.

- [Manage Subscriptions](/subscriptions-management/manage-subscriptions). Open and work with the subscription list.
- [Add New Subscription](/subscriptions-management/add-new-subscription)
- [Renew Subscription](/subscriptions-management/renew-subscription). Renew a subscription for a subscriber.
- [Request Payment](/subscriptions-management/request-payment). Email subscribers who haven't paid.
- [Resend Emails](/subscriptions-management/resend-emails). Send confirmation emails again.
- [Enable/Disable Reminders](/subscriptions-management/enable-disable-reminders). Control whether renewal reminders are sent.
- [Mass Mail Subscriptions](/subscriptions-management/mass-mail-subscriptions). Email selected subscribers.
- [Batch Subscriptions](/subscriptions-management/batch-subscriptions). Extend multiple subscriptions at once.
- [Export Subscriptions](/subscriptions-management/export-subscriptions). Export records to Excel or PDF.
- [Import Subscribers](/subscriptions-management/import-subscribers). Import existing subscriber records.
- [Report](/subscriptions-management/report). View subscriptions summarized by subscriber.
- [Export Expired Subscribers](/subscriptions-management/export-expired-subscribers)
- [Manage Subscribers](/subscriptions-management/manage-subscribers). Work with subscriber records and their subscriptions.
