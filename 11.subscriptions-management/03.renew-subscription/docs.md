title: 'Renew Subscription'
media_order: renew-subscription.png
---

If you want to renew certain subscriptions from the administrator area of your site, go to Membership Pro -> Subscriptions, click the checkbox next to subscription record(s) you want to renew, then click the **Actions -> Renew Subscription** button in the toolbar to process the subscription renewal. You can check on multiple checkboxes to process renewal for multiple subscriptions at a time
