### Chapter 3

# Frontend Items

Use these menu item types to display subscription plans, let users sign up, and give subscribers access to their account and membership options.

- [Categories](/frontend-items/categories). List plan categories.
- [Categories Accordion](/frontend-items/categories-accordion). Show categories and their plans in an accordion.
- [Plans](/frontend-items/plans). List plans from one or all categories.
- [Plan Details](/frontend-items/plan-details). Show the details of a single plan.
- [Subscription Form](/frontend-items/subscription-form). Link directly to the sign-up form for a plan.
- [User Profile](/frontend-items/user-profile). Give subscribers access to their profile and membership information.
- [Subscription History](/frontend-items/subscriptions-history). Show a subscriber their subscription records.
- [Renew Membership](/frontend-items/renew-membership). Show the available renewal options.
- [Upgrade Membership](/frontend-items/upgrade-membership). Show the available upgrade options.
- [Display Members List](/frontend-items/display-members-list). Display subscribers from one or more plans.
- [Manage Subscriptions](/frontend-items/manage-subscriptions). Give authorized users access to subscription management on the frontend.
- [User's Resources](/frontend-items/user-resources). Show resources available to subscribers.
- [QRCODE Scanner](/frontend-items/qrcode-scanner). Check membership status with the built-in scanner.
