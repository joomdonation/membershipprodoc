### Chapter 2

# Basic Setup

Set up subscription plans and forms, configure payments and taxes, and customize the messages and reminders subscribers receive.

- [Categories](/basic-setup/categories). Organize subscription plans into categories and subcategories.
- [Plans](/basic-setup/plans). Configure sign-ups, renewals, upgrades, and recurring billing.
- [Custom Fields](/basic-setup/custom-fields). Choose the information to collect on subscription forms.
- [Tax Rules](/basic-setup/tax-rules). Configure taxes for subscriptions.
- [Payment Plugins](/basic-setup/payment-plugins). Install and configure payment methods.
- [Coupons](/basic-setup/coupons). Offer discounts on subscriptions.
- [Emails & Messages](/basic-setup/emails-messages). Customize subscription messages and emails.
- [Invoicing Integration](/basic-setup/invoice-integration). Configure PDF invoices.
- [Setup Reminder](/basic-setup/setup-reminder). Send renewal reminders by email.
- [Membership Card](/basic-setup/membership-card). Offer downloadable PDF membership cards.
- [Enable Captcha](/basic-setup/enable-captcha). Protect subscription forms from spam.
- [GDPR](/basic-setup/gdpr). Review privacy-related settings.
- [Countries & States](/basic-setup/countries-states). Manage the countries and states offered on the subscription form.
- [Translation](/basic-setup/Translation). Edit and translate language strings.
- [SMS Reminders](/basic-setup/sms-reminders). Send renewal reminders by text message.
- [Conditional Text](/basic-setup/conditional-text). Show content in invoices and messages based on tag values.
- [Conversion Tracking Code](/basic-setup/conversion-tracking-code). Run tracking code after sign-up.
