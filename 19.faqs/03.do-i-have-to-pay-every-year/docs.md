---
title: 'Do I have to pay for the extension every year?'
visible: true
---

The answer is that depends on you. Whe you purchase the product, you will get free support and update from us for one site for one year. After one year:

- The extension will still work well on your site.
- If you don't want to continue getting support and update, you don't need to purchase it again.
- However, if you want to continue gettting support and update (to use new features and might be bugs fixes from new version), you need to renew your subscription (pay again)
