---
title: 'Create Confirm Email Field'
visible: true
---

You might want to create a field to allow users to re-type email to make sure they enter correct email. In this case, create a new field with following setting:

**Name**: confirm_email
**Title**: Confirm Email (or Retype Email or any title you want)
**Type**: Text
**Validation Rules** (most important): validate[equals[email]]
