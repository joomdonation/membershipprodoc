# Getting Started

Use subscription plans to grant access to restricted content on your Joomla site. Choose a method based on the type of resource you need to protect.

- [Use Joomla ACL](/resources-access-restriction/use-joomla-acl). Use Joomla user groups and access levels to protect items such as menus, modules, and articles.
- [Restrict access to Joomla articles](/resources-access-restriction/restrict-access-to-joomla-articles). Protect entire articles based on subscription plans.
- [Restrict access to part of article](/resources-access-restriction/restrict-access-to-part-of-article). Show an article's introduction publicly and restrict the full text.
- [Restrict access to K2 items](/resources-access-restriction/restrict-access-to-k2-items)
- [Restrict access to SP Page Builder Pages](/resources-access-restriction/restrict-access-to-sp-page-builder-pages)
- [Restrict access to urls](/resources-access-restriction/restrict-access-to-urls). Protect resources that you can identify by their URLs.
