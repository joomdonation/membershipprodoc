title: 'Getting Help'
---

If you have questions about setting up or using Membership Pro, you can reach us through any of the following support channels.

1. Search the [Membership Pro forum](http://joomdonation.com/forum/membership-pro) for your question. Another customer may have already received an answer. If you can't find one, open a new topic.
2. Log in to our website and submit a ticket in the **Membership Pro** category at [http://joomdonation.com/support-tickets.html](http://joomdonation.com/support-tickets.html).
3. Drop us an email to services@joomdonation.com
4. Use Contact Form at our website: [http://joomdonation.com/contact-us.html](http://joomdonation.com/contact-us.html)
5. Contact me directly via Skype for urgent support. My Skype nickname is **os.tuanpn**

We aim to respond within 24 hours on weekdays and within 48 hours on weekends.

>>>>> If you send us an email (to services@joomdonatiom.com or via contact form) but didn't receive our response, it is very likely that we missed your email for some reason. In that case, please do help by re-sending the email. We never ignore any customers, so it is unusual that we don't reply your email / support request.

___

>>>>> If we ask for more information in a support ticket, reply in the ticket to keep the conversation in one place. We may miss a separate email because of the volume of email we receive.

___

>>>>> Post questions about how the extension works in the forum. We'll answer there so other customers can see the response.

___

>>>>> If your support request is related to a bug in the extension which you found, please submit support ticket, that's the preferred way. When you submit support ticket, you should include super admin account of your site to reduce the time. For a bug fixing support request, it is very likely that we will have to edit code directly on your server, so sending super admin account to us when you submit ticket will save time of both of us
